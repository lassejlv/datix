1. Parties, acceptance and priority

You enter into this DPA when you accept the Terms from your verified Datix account. That record identifies the verified account email, the accepted versions, and the acceptance date and time. Datix offers and agrees to this DPA through that acceptance; a separate handwritten signature is not required. Downloading a blank copy or merely creating an account does not constitute acceptance.

You may also sign this DPA in account settings. That optional record adds your legal name, whether you act as controller or processor, and your authorized representative and their role. A sole trader enters their own legal name, and the representative confirms their authority to bind you. Signing does not change the terms of this DPA.

This DPA takes effect at the recorded acceptance time and continues for as long as Datix processes Customer Personal Data. It does not retrospectively authorize earlier processing. It prevails over conflicting service terms concerning data protection. An expressly agreed, separately signed DPA may replace this one. Mandatory data protection law and applicable international-transfer clauses prevail where they require greater protection.

2. Roles and scope

"Customer Personal Data" means personal data Datix processes on your behalf through your websites, environments, analytics, diagnostics, imports and related support. "GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679; its definitions of personal data, processing, controller, processor, data subject and personal data breach apply. This DPA addresses Article 28 and applicable EU and Danish data protection law. Pseudonymous or cookieless processing is not automatically anonymous processing.

You normally act as controller and Datix acts as processor. If you process data for another controller, Datix acts as your subprocessor: you must have that controller's authorization, pass on its lawful instructions and ensure this arrangement meets your obligations to it. Datix assists you so you can assist that controller. Your configured websites and environments define the scope of the service; unrelated entities do not become parties merely by using your account.

Datix separately acts as controller for its own account administration, contract acceptance records, billing administration, own-site analytics and business correspondence, as described in the Privacy Policy. This does not authorize Datix to reuse Customer Personal Data for independent marketing or profiling.

3. Instructions and customer responsibilities

Datix will process Customer Personal Data only on your documented instructions, including for transfers outside the EEA, unless EU or Member State law requires otherwise. In that case Datix will inform you before processing unless that law prohibits notification on important public interest grounds. The service agreement, this DPA, your settings, supported uploads and requests to our data protection contact document your instructions. Further instructions must be recorded in writing, including email. Datix will immediately inform you if it considers an instruction to infringe applicable EU or Member State data protection law and will suspend the affected instruction while the parties resolve it.

You determine the purposes of your analytics, establish an appropriate legal basis, provide visitor privacy information, and obtain and honor consent where required. You control which websites, pages, events and tracking modes to enable. Accepting this DPA is not a visitor's analytics consent and does not remove ePrivacy or cookie obligations. Ensure your uploads and instructions are lawful and keep personal or sensitive information out of paths, labels and event names. Do not use Datix to collect special-category data, criminal-offence data, passwords, payment-card details or government identifiers. Notify us promptly if such data is submitted inadvertently so we can restrict access and assist with removal.

Taking account of the nature of the processing, Datix will assist you through appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, in responding to requests under Chapter III of the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability. We do not require unnecessary identification data or promise that every visitor can be reidentified.

8. Personal data breaches

Datix will notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. Notification will go to your account contact and, where provided, your designated security contact. Keep those details current. Datix will describe the nature of the breach, affected categories and approximate numbers of people and records where known, likely consequences, mitigation taken or proposed, and a contact for follow-up. Information may be supplied in stages without undue further delay as the investigation develops.

Datix will take appropriate steps to contain and remedy the breach and cooperate with your response. You remain responsible for notifications required of you as controller; Datix's duty to notify you is not conditional on completion of an investigation or a finding of harm.

9. Duration, return and deletion

Processing continues while the service is provided and for the limited period needed to complete return or deletion. Annex A sets out the ordinary retention rules. You may instruct earlier deletion using the service controls or our contact address. At the end of the processing services, Datix will, at your choice, return or delete Customer Personal Data and delete existing copies, unless EU or Member State law requires storage. Request return before deleting an account; Datix will coordinate a usable electronic copy and explain available formats. A dashboard export button is not a condition of this assistance.

Deletion removes account access and schedules environment data for background cleanup. Datix will also address remaining queues, diagnostic recovery records, optional archives and provider copies within the scope of the instruction. Recovery copies that cannot immediately be erased will be isolated from ordinary use, protected under this DPA, and removed through the applicable recovery cycle; if restored, the deletion instruction will be reapplied. Datix will explain applicable completion periods and confirm completion on request. A technical backlog does not authorize indefinite retention. Data retained by a legal requirement will be restricted to that requirement, with the requirement and expected duration explained unless disclosure is prohibited.

Datix will make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 and this DPA, and allow and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by you or an auditor you mandate. Annex A — Processing description

  • Subject and purpose: website analytics, visitor estimates, activity reports, performance and error diagnostics, supported analytics imports, and related delivery, security, troubleshooting, return and deletion on your instructions.
  • Operations: receive, validate, minimize, pseudonymize, store, organize, aggregate, query, display to authorized accounts, transmit to approved providers, export and delete.
  • People: visitors to your tracked websites, including customers, prospects, employees or other users; people whose information occurs in authorized imports or support material. The tracker does not determine age, and your audience may include children.
  • Data: request IP address and bounded browser headers processed at collection; pseudonymous visitor and session keys; timestamps, paths, referrer hostnames, country, browser, operating system, device, language and display dimensions; configured events and labels, clicks, link destinations, downloads, form-submission events, scroll and active-time measurements; performance values and sanitized error diagnostics; normalized imported metrics, dimensions and import metadata. Paths and labels can themselves contain personal data.
  • Excluded content: the standard tracker does not collect typed form values, passwords, keystroke values, page text or full page contents, and does not provide video replay. Sensitive and criminal-offence data are not authorized, as explained in section 3.
  • Modes: cookieless grouping uses a keyed environment/day/IP/browser hash; optional cookie or local-storage identifiers can recognize browsers across visits and are converted to keyed environment-specific stored identifiers. Persistent browser visitor IDs expire after 90 days and session IDs after 30 minutes, renewed with activity. Cookieless mode also uses session storage for duplicate-pageview control. You are responsible for the applicable consent integration for your configuration.
  • Frequency: continuous or intermittent collection while your enabled integration runs; imports and support processing when you request them.
  • Ordinary retention: pageviews, custom events, daily visitor keys, goals and aggregate/imported reports use a rolling 730-calendar-day window. Detailed activity and application diagnostics are scheduled for deletion after approximately 30 days. Completed or rejected receipt identifiers are scheduled for deletion after approximately 731 days, with payloads cleared after processing. Detailed abuse records become eligible for cleanup after two days without an update; daily abuse summaries use approximately 90 days.
  • Other copies: pending receipts retain payloads until processed or deleted; failed diagnostic recovery records have no automatic fixed time expiry. Optional normalized import archives follow the report window. Provider recovery copies and logs can follow different cycles. Datix must handle these copies under section 9; the reporting window is not a promise of simultaneous erasure of every copy.

Annex B — Security measures

The application implements the following protections:

  • Account authentication, password hashing, signed session cookies and account-based authorization for websites and environments. Unverified and suspended accounts cannot access protected APIs.
  • HTTPS for the public service, TLS connections to the configured remote database and Redis, restricted database runtime credentials, bounded query timeouts and separate owner tooling.
  • Removal of query strings and URL fragments from tracker page and destination URLs; storage of referrer hostnames; keyed visitor identifiers; input validation, size limits and diagnostic redaction. Raw visitor IP addresses are not stored in analytics event tables or ordinary queue payloads. Infrastructure request processing and account security records remain separate.
  • Origin checks for account mutations, validation of tracked origins, rate limits, event deduplication, transactional queue processing and configured tracking exclusions.
  • Backend error monitoring configured to omit user profiles, request bodies, headers, cookies, IP addresses and query strings; no frontend Sentry SDK or Sentry session replay.
  • Scheduled retention and environment deletion through bounded cleanup, with recovery handling and further assistance under section 9.

Under this DPA, Datix must restrict and periodically review operator access, protect credentials, bind authorized personnel to confidentiality, maintain incident response and recovery procedures, review provider safeguards, and assess security measures regularly. Datix will maintain appropriate recovery arrangements and test restoration where relevant to the risks. These obligations do not represent a certification, a particular backup frequency, an uptime guarantee or a promise of absolute security.
